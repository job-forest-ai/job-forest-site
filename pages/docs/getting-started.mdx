# Getting Started

Welcome to JobForest! This guide will help you get started with our platform.

## Introduction

JobForest is a community platform that combines:
- **Learning resources** for skill development
- **Job opportunities** and career guidance
- **AI innovation** insights and trends

## Quick Setup

1. **Join the Community**
   - Sign up for an account
   - Complete your profile
   - Explore available resources

2. **Explore Features**
   - Browse learning materials
   - Check job listings
   - Connect with community members

3. **Get Involved**
   - Participate in discussions
   - Share your knowledge
   - Help others grow
